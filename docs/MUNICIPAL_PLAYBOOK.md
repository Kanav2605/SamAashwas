# Municipal Playbook: Deploying CivicSense AI in Indian ULBs

This playbook provides standard operating procedures (SOPs) for Municipal Commissioners, Chief Engineers, and Ward Officers rolling out CivicSense AI across cities.

---

## 1. Ward Administrative Hierarchy & Routing

| Role | Responsibility | Platform Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Municipal Commissioner / Smart City CEO** | City-wide strategic KPI oversight & flood risk mitigation | Predictive Monsoon Risk Index & Heatmaps |
| **Superintending Engineer (SE)** | Zonal grievance escalation & resource balancing | Cross-ward incident distribution & SLA breaches |
| **Ward Junior Engineer (JE / AE)** | Ground-level ticket resolution & contractor validation | Master Incident Triage & Inspection Queue |
| **Field Maintenance Crew / Safai Mitra** | Execution (pothole patching, desilting, bulb replacement) | Dispatched via automated Master Ticket job cards |

---

## 2. SLA Escalation Matrix

| Priority Level | Trigger Conditions | First Action SLA | Maximum Resolution SLA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | $\ge 15$ reports OR sparking wire / open manhole / road collapse | 30 minutes | 6 hours |
| **High** | $\ge 5$ reports OR arterial road pothole / major sewer overflow | 2 hours | 12 hours |
| **Medium** | Isolated single-citizen complaint (dark streetlight, trash heap) | 6 hours | 48 hours |
| **Low** | Aesthetic request, non-blocking sidewalk paint | 24 hours | 96 hours |

---

## 3. Pre-Monsoon Proactive Action Checklist

When the **Ward Vulnerability Index (WVI)** exceeds 65:
1. **Automated Alert:** SMS / WhatsApp notification triggered to the respective Ward JE and desilting contractor.
2. **Asset Inspection:** Priority inspection of SWD outfalls and culvert grates flagged with `HIGH_SILT_ACCUMULATION`.
3. **Equipment Pre-positioning:** Move high-capacity dewatering diesel pump sets to designated low-lying junctions before precipitation starts.
